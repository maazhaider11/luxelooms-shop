Privacy Policy

LuxeLooms · Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LuxeLooms ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our website (the "Storefront") and our social-media content automation application (the "App") that publishes product content to platforms including Pinterest, Facebook, and Instagram. By using our Storefront or authorizing our App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who We Are

LuxeLooms is an affiliate-marketing brand that curates and shares home & kitchen products. Our App automates the creation and publishing of promotional content (images, videos, and captions) about these products to social-media accounts that we own and control.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate on our own connected accounts and does not seek to collect personal information from the general public. Specifically:

  • Account access tokens: When we connect our own Pinterest, Facebook, or Instagram accounts, the respective platform issues us access tokens. These are stored securely and used only to publish content to our own accounts.
  • Product data: We retrieve publicly available product information (titles, images, prices, links) from the AliExpress Affiliate program to create posts.
  • Storefront visitors: Our Storefront is a static web page. We do not require accounts, do not run advertising trackers, and do not intentionally collect personal information such as names, emails, or payment details from visitors.
  • Standard server logs: Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically record basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability, as described in their own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and publish product posts to our own social-media accounts.
  • To generate captions and descriptions using automated tools.
  • To display curated products on our Storefront and link them to retailer pages.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App and Storefront interact with the following third parties, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Pinterest — to publish Pins to our account.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) — to publish posts and reels to our accounts.
  • AliExpress Affiliate Program — for product data and affiliate links.
  • Mistral AI — to generate post captions and descriptions.
  • GitHub Pages — to host the Storefront.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties.

5. Affiliate Disclosure

LuxeLooms participates in affiliate programs, including the AliExpress Affiliate program. Links to products on our Storefront and in our posts may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you make a purchase through them, at no additional cost to you.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our Storefront does not set advertising or analytics cookies. When you click a product link, the destination retailer (e.g., AliExpress) may set its own cookies to attribute the referral; this is governed by that retailer's privacy policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain access tokens only for as long as needed to operate the App, and product data only to power our Storefront. You may revoke our App's access to any connected account at any time through that platform's settings, which stops further use of the corresponding tokens.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we may hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
